# AI Team Formation
Team recommendation evaluates complexity, risk, capability coverage, permissions, availability, reliability, workload and cost.

Team types: SINGLE_AGENT, SPECIALIST_PAIR, CROSS_FUNCTIONAL, REVIEW_TEAM, RESEARCH_TEAM, ENGINEERING_TEAM, SALES_TEAM, SUPPORT_TEAM, SECURITY_TEAM, EMERGENCY_TEAM, CUSTOM.

A team is temporary unless a durable team contract exists. Leader coordinates but cannot override governance.
