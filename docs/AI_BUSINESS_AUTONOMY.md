# AI Business Autonomy

Autonomy levels: L0 Human Only, L1 AI Assist, L2 AI Execute + Approval, L3 AI Execute + Exception, L4 High Autonomy.

External action gate: intent → authorization → risk → policy → budget → idempotency → execute.

Emergency stops can target organization, workflow or narrower scopes. Every stop is audited with actor, reason and timestamp. High-risk actions prioritize quality and safety.
