# Workforce Resilience
Failure classes: TIMEOUT, TOOL_FAILURE, LOW_CONFIDENCE, QUALITY_FAILURE, SAFETY_FAILURE, PERMISSION_FAILURE, SYSTEM_FAILURE.

Recovery: detect → pause → assess → reassign/alternative strategy → resume → verify. Retries are bounded by max_attempts, cost/risk limits and escalation thresholds.

Critical roles should have primary, backup and successor coverage. Emergency teams remain subject to authorization and governance.
