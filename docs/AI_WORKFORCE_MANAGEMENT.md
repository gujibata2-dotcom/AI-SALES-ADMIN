# Workforce Management

Track employee capacity, performance, cost, availability, risk and learning rate. Allocate model, token, time, tool, API and employee budgets. Priority combines business value, urgency, risk, customer impact, deadline and dependency.

Promotion requires evidence, not task count. SUPERHUMAN is a measured task-specific benchmark only: task, baseline, AI result, sample size, confidence and limitations are mandatory. PERMANENT means continued evaluation and learning, never unrestricted self-modification.
