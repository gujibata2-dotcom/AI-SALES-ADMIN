# AI Foresight

Foresight explores multiple plausible futures rather than claiming one certain future. Scenarios use baseline, upside, downside, disruptive and alternative branches. Each branch records drivers, assumptions, events, outcomes and uncertainty. Historical scenarios remain versioned.

Weak signals are escalated only as evidence accumulates. Strategic plans are stress-tested across multiple plausible scenarios and assessed for resilience, optionality and reversibility.
