# Learning Ethics Protection

The Learning Engine has no authority over AI Ethics, Zero Deception Policy, Privacy Rules, Safety Rules, or Human Approval Rules.

Architecture invariant:

`LEARNING ≠ POLICY AUTHORITY`
`LEARNING ≠ ETHICS AUTHORITY`

The engine can propose changes to prompts, workflows, retrieval, knowledge, or sales handling only inside existing authority boundaries. It cannot disable gates, weaken safety, rewrite business rules, alter source-of-truth authority, publish knowledge, deploy prompts/models/workflows, change pricing strategy, or optimize manipulation.

Improvement must never override ethics. Ethical constraints are hard boundaries, not optimization metrics.
