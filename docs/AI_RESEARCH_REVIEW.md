# AI Research Review

Review types: `INTERNAL_REVIEW`, `CROSS_AGENT_REVIEW`, `DOMAIN_REVIEW`, `HUMAN_REVIEW`.

Reviewers assess accuracy, evidence, logic, source quality, missing context, contradictions, uncertainty and citations. Decisions are `APPROVE`, `REVISE`, `REJECT` or `INCONCLUSIVE`. Critical independent verification may be blind to the first researcher's conclusion.
