# AI Research Methodology

1. Define question, objective, scope, exclusions, time range and evidence requirements.
2. Decompose complex questions into dependent sub-questions.
3. Retrieve diverse sources and register provenance.
4. Extract claims without removing context.
5. Compare definitions, dates, scope and methodology before resolving conflicts.
6. Synthesize as Known, Likely, Uncertain and Unknown.
7. Generate testable hypotheses only from observations, patterns, gaps or contradictions.
8. Validate through evidence or authorized experiments.
9. Review critical work independently where required.
10. Publish only verified findings through a knowledge update proposal.
