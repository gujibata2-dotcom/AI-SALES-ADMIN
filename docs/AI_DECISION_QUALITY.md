# Decision Quality

Decision quality is assessed from evidence sufficiency, assumption reasonableness, risk identification, alternatives, authorization correctness, and execution correctness. Outcome quality is a separate signal.

Supported classifications:
- GOOD_DECISION_GOOD_OUTCOME
- GOOD_DECISION_BAD_OUTCOME
- BAD_DECISION_GOOD_OUTCOME
- BAD_DECISION_BAD_OUTCOME
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

Original context, evidence, assumptions, forecasts, options, and decision versions must remain available to prevent hindsight rewriting.
