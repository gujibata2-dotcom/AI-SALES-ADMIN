# Governance & Safety

## Human decision loop
AI detects -> AI recommends -> Human reviews -> Human approves/rejects -> System executes -> Verification -> Audit -> Learning.

## Hard boundaries
No self-approval, privilege escalation, policy mutation, governance bypass, audit deletion, fabricated evidence/metrics/forecasts, hidden negative evidence, or direct Decision Engine -> external action. Execution must go through Phase 20 Operations and Action Gateway.

## Critical decisions
Financial, legal, security, privacy, employment, irreversible, and high-risk customer-impact decisions require human approval. Low-confidence recommendations cannot execute critical actions.

## Truth model
Keep FACT, SIGNAL, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION separate. Correlation is a hypothesis, not causation. Simulations are estimates, not outcomes. Forecasts include confidence, data period, model version, and limitations.
