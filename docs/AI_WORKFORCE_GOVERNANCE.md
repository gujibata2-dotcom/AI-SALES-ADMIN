# Workforce Governance & Autonomy

Default autonomy is L2. L4 requires an explicit approver policy. Financial, legal, credential, policy, destructive and high-impact customer actions are human-gated unless the policy explicitly permits otherwise (and the default policy does not).

Every delegation, handoff, review, authorization, execution result and verification is recorded as a workforce event. Historical workflow versions are represented by immutable versioned records rather than overwriting the prior record.
