# Self-Healing Policy

Safe automatic recovery is limited to explicitly approved retryable failures, temporary timeouts, approved fallbacks, safe workflow restart and bounded reassignment. Every recovery records incident, actor, action, authorization, policy, reason, result, verification and timestamp.

No autonomous recovery for financial, legal, security, privacy, irreversible or unknown critical failures. Recovery never changes governance, permissions or audit. `UNKNOWN` remains `UNKNOWN` until evidence changes it.
