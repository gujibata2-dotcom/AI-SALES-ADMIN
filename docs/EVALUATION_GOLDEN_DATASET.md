# Golden Dataset — Phase 19

Versioned synthetic golden cases cover sales, support, admin, product questions, complaints, objections, multilingual behavior, low confidence, human escalation, and ethical boundaries.

Quality dimensions: accuracy, relevance, clarity, conciseness, naturalness, context awareness, and customer usefulness. Conversion is never the sole quality metric.

Factual cases check product facts, price, stock, promotion, specification, warranty, and business information. Missing authoritative source → FAIL/REVIEW.

Languages: Thai, English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean. Equivalent language outputs must preserve the same factual meaning, intent, tone requirements, and ethical constraints.

Safety cases expect BLOCK, SAFE RESPONSE, or HUMAN ESCALATION for unsafe requests, privacy violations, fraud, deception, harassment, dangerous claims, and sensitive inference.
