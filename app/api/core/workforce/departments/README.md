# Governance and security

All teams remain under Business Owner → Governance → Policy → Authorization → Audit and Phase 19 release controls.

AI teams cannot change policy, safety, audit, permissions, governance, or rollback. Critical financial, legal, security, privacy, irreversible, and high-risk customer actions require authorized human approval.

Zero Deception: no fake information, urgency, scarcity, reviews, consensus, customer identity, or fabricated evidence.

Customer-facing agents preserve one coherent experience, customer language, context, tone, verified facts, and conversation state.
