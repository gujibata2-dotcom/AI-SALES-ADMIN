# Organization model

Organization supports Business Owner → AI Operations Manager → Sales / Support / Content / Operations / Research teams → specialists. Role names are organizational abstractions only; hierarchy never grants permission.

Supported teams: Sales Team, Support Team, Content Team, Research Team, Operations Team.

A team may contain human employees, AI employees, and AI specialists. Managers may assign, prioritize, review, escalate, and coordinate within their authorization. AI managers cannot alter governance or increase their own permissions.

Track active tasks, queued tasks, blocked tasks, workload, and capacity. Overload triggers reassignment, queueing, or escalation.

Future specialist roles include Sales, Automotive, Real Estate, Travel, Product, Customer Support, and Content. All use Core Ethics, Governance, Authorization, Audit, and Safety.
